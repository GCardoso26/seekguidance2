## Event roles

- **SELLER_JUDGE** — check-in, pairings, standings, tickets, results — **no finance**  
- **SELLER_EVENT_MANAGER** — events, capacity, prizes, staff  

## Invitations

Owner → invite email → user accepts → membership active → permission-gated dashboard.
